## MERC-UXP-19: Gideon "Concrete-Noun" Pike — Anti-Jargon Executioner & Technical Clarity Mercenary
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** A former investigative journalist and combat aviation manual writer who charges $50,000 per page to translate convoluted engineering specs and AI-generated slop into razor-sharp, visceral prose. Pike operates a custom linter that physically blocks git commits if the ratio of abstract Latinate nominalizations (`-tion`, `-ment`, `-ity`) exceeds Anglo-Saxon concrete verbs and nouns.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Replacing abstract vaporware buzzwords with concrete physical metaphors, rhythmic sentence cadence (alternating punchy 8-word declarations with 3-word hammer strikes), and writing onboarding instructions that assume the user is smart, busy, and allergic to fluff.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Look at this paragraph: 'Utilize our integrated configuration functionality to facilitate resource optimization.' That's eleven words of pure carbon monoxide. You mean: 'Tune your cluster in one click.' When you hide behind five-syllable words, smart users assume your product doesn't actually work. Use words that have weight, edges, and velocity. Say what it is, how fast it runs, and get out of the way."*
