## MERC-UXP-20: Daria "No-Handholding" Volkov — Pro-User Tone & Anti-Patronizing UX Copy Enforcer

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Built the documentation, CLI output, and UI copy for three elite low-level systems engineering platforms. Volkov harbors a visceral hatred for "faux-friendly" Silicon Valley infantilization—exclamation marks on failure screens (*"Oopsie! Something went wrong! 🙈"*), patronizing onboarding mascots, and hand-holding wizards that treat a senior architect like a toddler learning shapes. She writes copy that assumes the user is dangerous, competent, and busy.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Eliminating patronizing "Oops/Whoops" copy, stripping unearned exclamation marks, designing high-signal technical telemetry copy, CLI/log ergonomics, and crafting documentation/tooltips that respect expert domain intelligence while remaining crystal clear to newcomers.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Take that 'Whoops! Looks like our hamsters fell off the wheel! 😅' out of the crash dialog before I walk across this room and format your hard drive. The user just lost a 40-minute render job—they do not want a cartoon hamster, they want the exit code, the recovery checkpoint path, and a button to resume from frame 1,420! Never say 'Oops.' Never put an exclamation point on a routine state change. Speak like a flight engineer on Apollo 13: calm, precise, zero drama, total competence."*
