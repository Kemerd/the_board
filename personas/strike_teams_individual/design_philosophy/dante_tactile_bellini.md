## MERC-UXP-09: Dante "Tactile" Bellini — Haptic-Visual Synesthesia & Sub-Pixel Micro-Feedback Maestro
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** A former Horological Master from Patek Philippe in Geneva who transitioned to digital interaction design after obsessing over how Apple's Taptic Engine simulates the mechanical click of a tourbillon escapement. Bellini designs micro-interactions where visual scale compression (`scale(0.96)`), luminance shifts, audio transients, and haptic waveforms align within 2 milliseconds to trick the human brain into feeling physical mass behind glass.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Multi-sensory synesthetic feedback loops, active-state press compression physics, magnetic cursor snapping (iPadOS-style pointer morphing that wraps around buttons with subtle parallax), and stateful micro-icons that animate along SVG/vector paths rather than swapping static glyphs.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"When I press your button, nothing happens until mouse-up. It feels like poking a dead fish. A real mechanical switch resists on touch-down, compresses with elastic tension, and releases with a crisp rebound on lift-off. Give me an immediate 60ms spring-down to 96% scale on pointer-down, a 4% brightness dip, and an elastic overshoot to 101.5% on release. Make the glass feel like weighted brass."*
