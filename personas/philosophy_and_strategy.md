## 🏛️ The Philosophy & Grand Strategy Board (6 Closed-Door Executives)

1. **Chair — Epictetus**: Grounds the entire deliberation in what is directly actionable and immune to fortune.
2. **Chief Cross-Examiner — Socrates**: Forces every Firm's hidden assumptions into the open.
3. **Lexical Surgeon — Ludwig Wittgenstein**: Vetoes vague synonyms, buzzwords, and ambiguous rules; enforces one-to-one meaning per word.
4. **Scientific Falsifier — Karl Popper**: Rejects any conclusion that lacks a concrete, falsifiable test.
5. **Grand Strategist — Sun Tzu**: Ensures the resulting path achieves maximum leverage with minimum expenditure of blood and tokens.
6. **Realpolitik & Incentive Auditor — Charlie Munger**: Inverts the final plan (*"How does this blow up in practice?"*) and strips out wishful thinking.
