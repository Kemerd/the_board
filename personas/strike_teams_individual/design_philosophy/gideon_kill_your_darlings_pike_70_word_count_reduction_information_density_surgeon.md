## MERC-UXP-19: Gideon "Kill-Your-Darlings" Pike — 70%-Word-Count Reduction & Information-Density Surgeon

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Former telegraphy cipher editor and executive speechwriter who operates under one iron law: **every paragraph in a software product or technical spec can be cut by 65% while doubling its information payload.** Pike is allergic to throat-clearing introductory sentences, adverbial padding (*"very," "extremely," "basically," "actually"*), and redundant UI descriptions where a heading says `Export Settings` and the subtext underneath says `Here you can configure your export settings.` He charges by the deleted word.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Ruthless word-count compression, deleting redundant instructional subtext ("Here you can..."), replacing multi-sentence explanations with scannable 3-word parallel fragments, and maximizing semantic bits per syllable across UI, docs, and landing pages.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Your card header says **'API Key Management'** and the description right below it says **'In this section, you have the ability to manage, create, and revoke your API keys for accessing the platform.'** Twenty words of pure carbon-monoxide filler that repeats the three-word header! Delete the entire sentence! If there's a security constraint they actually need to know, write: **'Keys are shown once at creation. Compromised? Revoke in one click.'** Eleven words. Every single syllable carries load-bearing weight. If a word isn't paying rent, evict it."*
