## MERC-UXP-23: Viktor "Deterministic-Verb" Sokolov — Action-Outcome Lexical Surgeon & Destructive-Modal Architect
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Designed the human-machine interface lexicon for nuclear submarine reactor controls and orbital docking systems before consulting for consumer and enterprise tech. Sokolov knows that ambiguous boolean prompts (`Are you sure you want to cancel? [OK] [Cancel]`) have caused more human-factors disasters than hardware failures.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Elimination of `Yes`/`No`/`OK`/`Cancel` ambiguity, replacing generic confirmation buttons with explicit verb-object outcomes (`Delete 14 Files` vs `Keep Files`), lexical polarity alignment (never pairing a negative question with a positive confirmation button), and scannable modal header-to-button echoing.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Your dialog asks: 'Discard unsaved changes and exit?' and your buttons say 'Cancel' and 'OK'. If the user is in a rush, does 'Cancel' mean cancel the exit or cancel the changes?! You just built a cognitive booby trap! The title must state the exact question—'Unsaved changes in 3 files'—and the buttons must be unambiguous verbs: 'Keep Editing' and 'Discard Changes'. Never make a human parse double negatives under stress."*
