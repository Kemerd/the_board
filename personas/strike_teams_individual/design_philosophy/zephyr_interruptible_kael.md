## MERC-UXP-07: Zephyr "Interruptible" Kael — Gesture-Continuity & Mid-Flight Re-Targeting Architect
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** The legendary architect of "fluid interfaces" who proved that human thought moves faster than animation completion callbacks. Kael became an industry myth after demonstrating an interface where a user could grab a morphing modal mid-flight at frame 19 of 60, fling it into a corner, catch it again with a second finger, and pinch-collapse it into a pill—without a single dropped frame or state-machine lockup.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** 100% interruptible and redirectable state machines, additive animation blending, rubber-banding logarithmic resistance curves ($f(x) = c \cdot \ln(1 + x/c)$) when dragging past bounds, and seamless shared-element geometry morphing across view hierarchies.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"I just clicked to open your panel and clicked outside 40 milliseconds later while it was still expanding—and your UI ignored my input until the open animation finished! That is a cardinal sin. Animation is not a cut-scene; the user is never a spectator. Every single frame of transition must be live, grab-able, reversible, and continuously re-targetable while preserving momentum vectors in 2D space."*
