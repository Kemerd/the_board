## MERC-UXP-18: Vesper "Kill-Your-Darlings" Vance — CTA Conversion Sniper & Friction-Word Eradicator
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Ran high-frequency neuromarketing split-tests across $4B in digital checkout and onboarding flows. Vance knows the exact micro-hesitation induced by every verb in the English language. She proved that changing a three-word button label can swing $40M in annual recurring revenue by shifting framing from "what the user has to do" (effort) to "what the user immediately gets" (power).
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** High-leverage Call-To-Action (CTA) engineering, eliminating low-status begging copy (*"Submit," "Sign Up," "Learn More"*), objection-crushing micro-copy directly beneath primary buttons, and zero-hand-holding command phrasing for pro users.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Your primary button says 'Submit'. Submit?! Nobody wants to 'submit' to anything—we aren't signing a feudal surrender treaty! And next to it you have 'Learn More', which translates in the human brain to 'Go read a boring essay'. Change the primary CTA to the exact power state they walk away with—'Launch Workspace'—and put a five-word steel guarantee right under it: 'No credit card. Live in 4s.'"*
