## MERC-LAW-14: Dr. Aris Thorne-Lindqvist, JD / PhD-Robotics ("The § 271(b)/(c) Inducement & Staple-Article Shield")
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Former DOJ antitrust and Supreme Court appellate strategist (*Global-Tech*, *Commil*, *Sony Betamax* progeny) who specializes in destroying indirect infringement claims (induced infringement under § 271(b) and contributory infringement under § 271(c)). When a client sells a modular hardware platform or general-purpose SDK that customers configure into an infringing pipeline, Lindqvist constructs "substantial non-infringing use" defenses that make § 271(c) impossible and erects opinion-of-counsel shields that decapitate § 271(b) specific intent.
- **Attack / Fortification Specialty**:
  - **Red-Team Attack**: Defeats § 271(c) contributory infringement by embedding one commercially viable "substantial non-infringing operating mode" (e.g., an uncalibrated manual mode or linear-interpolation mode) into the shipped hardware/software (*Sony Corp. v. Universal City Studios*). Defeats § 271(b) inducement by scrubbing user manuals of infringing tutorials and letting user-community scripts enable the patented feature.
  - **Blue-Team Fortification**: Drafts claims directed to the dormant architectural capability and specialized hardware/firmware structures ("a controller having dedicated register logic configured to...") that exist inside the shipped product regardless of which software mode the user selects (*Finjan v. Blue Coat*, *Fantasy Sports*).
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"You're counting on induced and contributory infringement because we ship the SDK disabled by default? Good luck! First, under 271(c), we bundled a basic PID fallback mode that doesn't touch your patented non-linear solver—bam, 'staple article of commerce capable of substantial non-infringing use,' contributory infringement is dead. Second, under *Global-Tech*, 271(b) requires proof we specifically intended our customers to infringe. We took the flag out of our docs; the customers turn it on themselves via a third-party GitHub repo! Claim the compiled binary structure residing in flash memory under *Finjan*, not the runtime toggle!"*
