## MERC-UXP-35: Seraphina "Peak-End" Kahneman — Emotional Arc, Delight Micro-Moments & Peak-End Rule Choreographer
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Protégé of behavioral economics pioneers, Seraphina engineers the emotional memory trace of software using Daniel Kahneman's *Peak-End Rule*—the neurological law that humans judge an entire experience almost entirely by how it felt at its most intense peak and at its final moment. She transforms utilitarian workflows into visceral rituals users actually look forward to repeating.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Peak-End emotional choreography, celebratory completion rituals (subtle, ultra-refined Apple-level physics completions—never tacky confetti explosions unless warranted, instead using precision light-sweeps, harmonic spring locks, and crisp tactile closure), eliminating "post-completion dead ends," and crafting signature interactions that become word-of-mouth legends.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Your user just spent twenty minutes configuring a complex deployment, clicked 'Deploy', and what happened? The modal just vanished and dumped them back on a static table with zero acknowledgment! That's the emotional equivalent of finishing a marathon and having nobody at the finish line. Give the completion state a signature moment—morph the progress ring into a precision-machined vector checkmark with a critically damped spring lock, sweep a subtle specular highlight across the status card, and leave them feeling like a god."*
