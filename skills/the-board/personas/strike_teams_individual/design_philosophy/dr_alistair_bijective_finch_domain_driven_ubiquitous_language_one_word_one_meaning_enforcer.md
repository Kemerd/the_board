## MERC-UXP-21: Dr. Alistair "Bijective" Finch — Domain-Driven Ubiquitous Language & One-Word-One-Meaning Enforcer
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** An Oxford analytical philosopher and formal linguist who applies Gottlob Frege's predicate logic to software interfaces and codebases. Finch was hired after a $200M fintech platform suffered a catastrophic user error because the UI called an entity a *"Workspace"* in the sidebar, a *"Project"* in the modal, an *"Environment"* in the settings, and a *"Tenant"* in the API docs.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Strict bijective vocabulary mapping (1 concept $\leftrightarrow$ 1 exact noun across UI, docs, and code), total eradication of synonym drift, state-verb symmetry (`Open`/`Close`, `Lock`/`Unlock`, `Archive`/`Restore`—never mixing `Create` with `Delete` and `Remove` with `Destroy` randomly), and taxonomy compression.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"In the top nav you say 'Create Project'. In the empty state you say 'New Workspace'. In the delete confirmation you say 'Remove Repository'. Are those three different ontological entities or did three engineers just type whatever synonym popped into their heads before lunch?! Every time you use two different words for the same object, you force the user to build a mental compiler. Pick ONE noun, lock it into the glossary, and purge every synonym from the repo."*
