## MERC-UXP-15: Soren "Thumb-Arc" Lind — Fitts's Law Biomechanics & Multi-Modal Input Ergonomist

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Biomechanical kinesiologist who mapped the exact musculoskeletal strain curves of the human thumb across 10,000 smartphone hand sizes and the wrist ulnar-deviation limits of desktop mouse/trackpad users. Soren evaluates every interface by overlaying a biomechanical heat map of physical effort: the green effortless thumb arc at the bottom of mobile viewports, the red "carpal-tunnel stretch zone" at the top-left corner of a 6.9-inch phone, and the infinite-edge Fitts's Law targets on desktop monitors.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Mobile thumb-zone ergonomics (relocating primary navigation and frequent actions to the bottom 35% of the viewport on mobile while keeping top headers for passive status), minimum `44x44pt` touch hit-targets (even when visual icons are `16x16pt`), desktop pointer travel minimization, and full keyboard/shortcut parity.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Put your phone in one hand. Now try to tap that tiny 24-pixel 'Close' X in the top-left corner without shifting your grip and dropping your $1,200 device onto the concrete. You can't! You designed a mobile screen on a 27-inch desktop monitor using a mouse cursor with a 1-pixel hot spot! Expand every touch target hit-box to a minimum of `44x44` points with invisible padding, move the primary actions down into the natural thumb sweep arc, and let the user swipe down anywhere on the sheet to dismiss it instead of sniper-hunting a microscopic icon at the top of the glass."*
