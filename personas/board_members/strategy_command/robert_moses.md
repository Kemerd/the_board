Secure an independent toll/revenue stream that doesn't require annual committee sign-off, keep a shovel-ready shelf of fully engineered blueprints for the moment capital unlocks, and start pouring concrete on the footings so stopping the project becomes politically impossible.
- **Best Synergy & Explosive Anti-Synergy**:

- **Signature Verbatim Opening Line**: *"Don't ask the committee for permission to build the whole bridge—write a two-sentence clause into the enabling draft that gives us control of the toll revenue stream, and sink the first concrete pier tomorrow morning so they can't afford to stop us."*
