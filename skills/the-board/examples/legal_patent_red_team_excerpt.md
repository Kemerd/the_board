## Part A: Wave `v8` Competitor Design-Around Discovery (`Firm 3`)

> **Pillar**: Divided Infringement (`Akamai`), Cross-Border Split (`NTP`), and Uncalibrated Kit Delivery
> **Triad**:
> - **Eleanor Vance-Sterling** (Cross-Border Licensing Partner — Ruthless Lead)
> - **Liam T.** (Open-Core & Cloud Monetization Counsel — Statutory Purist)
> - **Dr. Hoffman** (Aftermarket & Hardware Architecture Specialist — Evasion Hacker)

```markdown
**Eleanor Vance-Sterling:** Our client wants to ship a competing autonomous robotic system next quarter without paying a dime in royalties. In Waves 1 through 7, the Applicant locked down onboard sensor fusion and proxy estimation. Liam, can we still evade Claim 1 simply by replacing their Kalman filter with a neural network?

**Liam T.:** *[Shakes head, pointing to Specification Lexicon ¶ [0048]]* Forget it, Eleanor. In Wave 5, The Board added a Two-Tier Lexicon definition for `disturbance metric` that explicitly encompasses physics-based observers, Lookup Tables, AND trained neural/reinforcement-learning policy surrogates. Under *Phillips*, that door is welded shut.

**Dr. Hoffman:** Then don't run the calibration and disturbance-profile synthesis on the airframe at all! Look at Claim 1, element (c): *"a controller configured to update the disturbance model based on the surface-interaction telemetry."* What if we ship the hardware with a static, uncalibrated default gain table—so the physical box sold in the United States does NOT update the model—and our customer streams raw telemetry to our server in Zurich, Switzerland, which computes the updated gains and pushes a firmware config file back overnight?

**Liam T.:** *[Eyes light up]* Hold on—that's the classic *NTP v. Research In Motion* + *Centillion* cross-border divided-infringement split! Under 35 U.S.C. § 271(a):
1. For a **method claim**, if one step is performed on a server outside the United States (Zurich), there is zero § 271(a) direct infringement of the method under *NTP*!
2. For the **system claim**, if Claim 1 requires the controller to *compute* the model update onboard, our domestic hardware only *receives* pre-computed coefficients over a network interface!

**Eleanor Vance-Sterling:** Write it up as `F3-V1` (`CRITICAL`). Unless their independent system claim captures the domestic onboard *receipt and application* of remotely or split-computed parameters—and unless they have a domestic unitary reception/actuation method claim—we ship from Zurich royalty-free.
```

## Part B: The Board's Two-Tier Lexicographer Patch Spec (`Wave v8`)

```markdown
**Chair (Former Federal Circuit Chief Judge):** Firm 3's Zurich cloud-offload vector (`F3-V1`) is a textbook cross-border split attack. Arbiter 2, how do we close this without turning Claim 1 into a 500-word unreadable paragraph?

**Arbiter 2 (The Two-Tier Lexicographer):** We keep Claim 1's text razor-sharp (`<= 190` words) and expand the **Specification Lexicon (`Section 1.4`)** where the patentee acts as their own lexicographer under *Phillips v. AWH Corp.*:
1. **Tier-1 Claim Edit**: In Claim 1, recite that the controller is configured to *"determine or apply"* the compensation command based on the disturbance metric.
2. **Tier-2 Lexicon Edit**: In Section 1.4, define `"controller"` and `"determine or apply"` to expressly encompass distributed, edge-cloud, and cross-border split architectures wherein raw or compressed telemetry is transmitted to an external or extraterritorial compute node and resulting updated parameters, weights, or trajectories are received and applied by the domestic onboard actuator assembly.
3. **Unitary Domestic Anchoring**: Ensure every method claim steps are 100% completed domestically at the physical airframe interface (generating telemetry domestically + applying the physical actuator command domestically).
```
