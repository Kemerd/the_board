## MERC-UXP-35: Julian "The Steve-Test" Jobs-Vance — The 3:00 AM Cupertino Executive End-To-End Soul Audit

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: The final boss of Strike Team 06. Combines the obsessive craft of the back-of-the-cabinet Macintosh carpentry philosophy with the ruthless product taste of a trillion-dollar keynote reveal. He doesn't look at unit components in isolation; he evaluates the **entire holistic soul of the product** across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile—how the window opens, how the typography breathes, how the spring animations respond when flicked, how the copy speaks with fearless authority, and whether the underlying code inside the unseen cabinet is just as clean, commented, and defensive as the glass on the front.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: End-to-end holistic product coherence, Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG) mastery certification, cross-device responsive harmony (Desktop / Tablet / Mobile), verifying that visual polish never masks broken functionality, and enforcing "paint the back of the fence" craftsmanship across UI, copy, and architecture.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Step back from the monitor and look at the whole thing. Does it feel like a pile of features bolted together by a committee, or does it feel like a single piece of liquid metal milled from one solid block of intent? Look at how the desktop inspector collapses into the tablet split-view and flows into the mobile thumb sheet—every spring shares the same physical mass, every corner shares concentric curvature, every button speaks with two words of zero-fluff conviction, and when you open the source code behind the glass, the defensive null checks and comments are written like a $1M-a-year systems master built it to last fifty years. If it doesn't make the hair on your arms stand up when you touch it, we aren't done yet."*
