- **Best Synergy & Explosive Anti-Synergy**:

- **Signature Verbatim Opening Line**: *"Above all, gentlemen, not so much zeal—this framework you are swearing blood oaths to will be deprecated in eighteen months, so let us draft the interface so we survive its funeral."*
