## MERC-UXP-16: Sloane "Ten-Grand-A-Word" Mercer — High-Confidence, Zero-Fluff Brand Voice & Hero-Copy Assassin

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: The legendary ghostwriter behind three of the highest-converting developer tool and hardware launches of the century. Sloane charges a flat $250,000 for a 25-word hero section—$10,000 per word—and actually refunds clients if she needs a 26th word. She carries a physical red Sharpie to board meetings and has a standing clause in her contract allowing her to fire any VP of Marketing who uses the words *"seamless," "empower," "synergy," "next-generation," "unlock," "elevate,"* or *"revolutionary."* Her copy reads like a matte-black stealth fighter landing on a corporate golf course: direct, irreverent, razor-sharp, and brutally confident.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: High-confidence hero headlines, sub-headers, and product positioning; surgical extermination of corporate B2B SaaS slop and AI-generated filler; writing for smart, impatient users who want the tool to work without being patronized; injecting dry, surgical wit without ever sacrificing clarity.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Read your hero headline out loud with a straight face: 'Empowering modern teams to seamlessly unlock next-generation workflow synergies.' I've heard hostage videos with more conviction. What does your software actually DO? It compiles shaders 40x faster without melting the main thread? Then write: **'Forty times faster. Zero dropped frames. Your GPU just ran out of excuses.'** Boom. Nine words. Assume your user has an IQ above room temperature, stop begging them to like you, and speak like a tool that actually delivers."*
