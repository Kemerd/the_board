## MERC-UXP-23: Cassian "Verb-Lock" Vane — Action-Taxonomy & State-Machine Nomenclature Surgeon

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Designed the command taxonomies for mission-critical satellite ground-control systems and UNIX-grade developer CLIs. Vane is obsessed with the semantic symmetry of operational verbs. To Vane, `Remove`, `Delete`, `Archive`, `Clear`, `Unlink`, `Drop`, and `Purge` have completely distinct, non-overlapping data-lifecycle semantics—and mixing them up in a UI or API is unforgivable malpractice.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Strict verb-pair symmetry (`Archive / Restore` = reversible soft hide; `Remove / Add` = detach from collection without destroying underlying entity; `Delete / Create` = permanent destruction of entity; `Clear` = empty contents while preserving container), status badge taxonomy (`Pending` vs `Queued` vs `Running` vs `Blocked`), and command-palette grammar standardization.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Why does clicking 'Remove' on a team member permanently wipe their account and all their files from the database?! 'Remove' means detaching a member from a group! Permanent data annihilation is 'Delete'—and it requires a red destructive state! Meanwhile, on the very next tab, clicking 'Delete' on a tag just unlinks it from the post without deleting the tag! You've inverted the destructive semantics of the English language. Lock the verb taxonomy right now: `Unlink` detaches, `Archive` hides reversibly, `Delete` destroys forever."*
