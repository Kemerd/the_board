## [02] SYNERGY & VOLATILE ANTI-SYNERGY DYNAMICS

- **Epictetus vs. Machiavelli**: Epictetus demands unshakeable internal virtue and immunity to fortune; Machiavelli points out how ruthless institutional actors will exploit naive idealism in the wild.
- **Wittgenstein vs. Socrates**: Socrates untangles sloppy definitions by questioning; Wittgenstein demands that every remaining word map 1-to-1 to a logical state of affairs (*Tractatus*).
- **Popper's Razor**: Vetoes any strategic or scientific claim that cannot specify what observable outcome would prove it wrong.
