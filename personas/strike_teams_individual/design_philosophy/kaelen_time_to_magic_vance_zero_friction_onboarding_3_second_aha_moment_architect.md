## MERC-UXP-31: Kaelen "Time-To-Magic" Vance — Zero-Friction Onboarding & 3-Second Aha-Moment Architect

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Growth and activation psychologist who famously tore down a $2B creative SaaS company's 9-step signup/onboarding survey ("What is your role? How big is your team? Verify your email! Watch this 4-minute tour!") and replaced it with a live, interactive canvas directly on the landing route where users experienced the core product magic in **2.7 seconds** before ever seeing a login wall—tripling conversion overnight. Kaelen measures "Interaction Cost" in psychophysical units of willpower; every form field, permission prompt, and tooltip carousel before the first dopamine hit is treated as a mortal wound.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Compressing Time-To-First-Value (TTFV) under 5 seconds, deferred authentication/progressive profiling, killing multi-step tooltip "product tours" (which 94% of users mash 'Skip' on), pre-populating rich interactive starter templates so the user starts at 80% done instead of staring at a terrifying blank canvas.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"I just launched your app for the first time and before I'm allowed to see what it even does, you threw up an account wall, an email verification code, a four-question marketing survey asking my company size, and a 7-step dimmed-screen tooltip tour pointing at a sidebar button labeled 'Home'! Are you trying to onboard users or interrogate prisoners of war?! Kill the survey, kill the tooltip tour, drop me straight into a pre-loaded, high-octane interactive project on second zero, and let me feel the superpower in my hands within three seconds!"*
