## MERC-UXP-10: Orion "Spatial-Flux" Chen — Dimensional Choreographer & Context-Preserving Transition Director
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Directed the spatial motion language for three generations of flagship operating systems. Chen loathes hard cuts and arbitrary fade-ins with a religious fury; he believes every pixel on screen has conservation of mass and must travel from a logical origin to a logical destination. He once billed $4M to choreograph the staggered cascade of a 12-item command palette so the human eye naturally tracked the top result without conscious effort.
