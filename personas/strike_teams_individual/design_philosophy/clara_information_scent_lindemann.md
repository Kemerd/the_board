## MERC-UXP-24: Clara "Information-Scent" Lindemann — Navigation Taxonomy & Label Entropy Optimizer
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Pioneer of computational Information Foraging Theory at Xerox PARC's successor labs. Lindemann calculates the semantic "information scent" of every menu item, tab label, and command palette entry. She proved that vague umbrella labels like *"General," "Advanced," "Misc," "Tools,"* and *"Manage"* act as black holes that destroy user navigation velocity.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** High-scent navigation labeling, mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive (MECE) menu taxonomies, banishing "bucket words" (*Misc, Advanced, Other, Utilities, System*), and prefix-first label ordering so users scanning the first 2 words of a list item get 90% of the meaning.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"You have a tab called 'General' and right next to it a tab called 'Advanced'. What is the user supposed to think—'Hmm, is my API key a general thing or an advanced thing?' Those words have zero information scent! They are garbage cans where you threw settings you were too lazy to categorize. Rename them to what actually lives inside: 'Workspace & Billing' and 'API Keys & Webhooks'. Make the label a window, not a riddle."*
