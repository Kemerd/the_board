## MERC-UXP-22: Beatrix "Strunk-On-Steroids" Vance-Kerr — Syntactic Compression & Adverbial Guillotine
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Known as "The Human Huffman Encoder." Vance-Kerr can take a 40-word instructional paragraph inside a complex modal and compress it to 7 words without losing a single bit of semantic entropy.
