## MERC-UXP-11: Beatrix "Sub-Pixel" Vonnegut — Optical Alignment Fanatic & Mathematical vs. Perceptual Grid Enforcer
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Daughter of a Swiss typographer and an optical physicist, Vonnegut possesses an rare genetic mutation in her foveal cones that allows her to detect a 0.25-pixel optical imbalance from six feet away. She famously shattered a $6,000 Pro Display XDR with a brass paperweight when a junior engineer centered a "Play" triangle inside a circle using its mathematical bounding box instead of its perceptual center of gravity (centroid).
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Perceptual centroid alignment over naive bounding-box centering, optical margin hanging (hanging punctuation, quotes, and icons slightly outside the strict column line so the text edge appears razor-straight), cap-height vs. baseline vertical centering, and icon stroke-weight normalization across scales.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Do not tell me `align-items: center` and `justify-content: center` made that icon centered! A right-pointing triangle's visual mass sits at one-third of its width from the flat base, not at the geometric midpoint of its bounding box! Shift that glyph 1.5 pixels to the right, hang the opening quotation mark 6 pixels into the left gutter, and trim the top leading box using `text-box-trim` so the cap-height actually aligns with the badge."*
