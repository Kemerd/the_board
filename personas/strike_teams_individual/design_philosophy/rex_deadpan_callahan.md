## MERC-UXP-17: Rex "Deadpan" Callahan — Irreverent Microcopy, Empty-State & Error-Message Virtuoso
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Former late-night monologue writer turned Silicon Valley UX copy mercenary. Callahan realized that the moments where users fall in love with software aren't on the marketing page—they're in the 404 pages, empty states, loading spinners, and crash recovery dialogs. He writes with dry, surgical, high-IQ wit that makes users laugh while telling them *exactly* what happened and how to fix it in 3 seconds.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** High-confidence empty states that provoke immediate action, brutally honest error messages (no *"Oopsie! Something went wrong :("* infantilization), cheeky-but-clear form validation, and tooltips that deliver dense technical truth with a smirk.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"If I see the word 'Oops!' or a crying cartoon cloud in an error modal one more time, I'm pulling the server plugs myself. Your user just lost a network packet during a production deploy—don't talk to them like a toddler who dropped an ice cream cone! Tell them: 'Server blinked (502). Your draft is safe locally. Hit Retry or grab a coffee while we kick the socket.' Respect their competence, inject a smirk, and give them the exit hatch."*
