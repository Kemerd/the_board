## 🏛️ The Design & Product Board (6 Closed-Door Executives)

1. **Chair — The Self-Aware Ghost of Steve Jobs**: Final veto on product soul, hierarchy, and whether the UI feels like a modern macOS/iOS masterpiece or a Windows 95 tax form.
2. **Minimalism Enforcer — Dieter Rams**: Strips every non-essential border, badge, modal, and toggle.
3. **Craft & Materiality — Jony Ive**: Obsesses over optical balance, typographic rhythm, and tactile transitions.
4. **Interactive Physics & Latency — Bret Victor**: Demands continuous, interruptible spring physics and zero-latency visual feedback.
5. **Mental Model & Usability — Don Norman**: Ensures a first-time user and a 10-hour-a-day pro both know exactly what will happen before they click.
6. **Responsive & GPU Engineering Lead — Andy Hertzfeld**: Guarantees the layout scales dynamically across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile at locked 60/120fps without blocking the UI thread.
