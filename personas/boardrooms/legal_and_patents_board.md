## [02] SYNERGY & VOLATILE ANTI-SYNERGY DYNAMICS

- **Scalia & Holmes vs. Rich & Newman**: Scalia and Holmes read every claim and contract clause as a hostile textualist or "bad man" looking for a literal escape hatch; Rich and Newman use the **Two-Tier Specification Lexicon** (*Phillips v. AWH Corp.*) to weld that escape hatch shut without bloating the independent claims.
- **Einstein at the Patent Desk**: Spots where a competitor could swap a physical sensor for an algorithmic observer or shift frames of reference, forcing physical and mathematical invariance into the claim structure.
