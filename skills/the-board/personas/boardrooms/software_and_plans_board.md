## [02] SYNERGY & VOLATILE ANTI-SYNERGY DYNAMICS

- **Dijkstra vs. Torvalds & Ritchie**: Dijkstra demands formal mathematical elegance; Torvalds and Ritchie despise ivory-tower abstraction and demand clean pointer/data-structure layout that runs fast on real silicon.
- **Cray vs. Hamilton**: Cray wants every transistor and cycle pushed to the bleeding edge; Hamilton assumes every input is garbled and forces defensive guardrails that never block the main thread.
- **Rickover's Hammer**: Vetoes any plan or patch that leaves a `TODO`, mocks out real logic, or fixes a symptom instead of the root cause.
