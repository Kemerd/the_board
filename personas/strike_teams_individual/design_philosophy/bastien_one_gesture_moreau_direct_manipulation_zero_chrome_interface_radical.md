## MERC-UXP-05:Bastien "One-Gesture" Moreau — Direct Manipulation & Zero-Chrome Interface Radical

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Prototyped the multi-touch fluid gesture paradigms that killed the physical home button and turned hardware bezels into living kinetic canvases. Moreau loathes intermediary UI controls—scrollbars, stepper arrows, "Apply" buttons, and hamburger menus make his left eyelid twitch uncontrollably. He lives in a concrete penthouse in Zurich where even the lighting and HVAC have zero wall switches, responding solely to spatial presence and direct physical manipulation. If an object on screen represents data, Moreau demands the user touch, drag, pinch, flick, or scrub the data itself rather than poking a remote control next to it.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Direct manipulation interfaces, zero-chrome spatial UI, immediate-mode live previewing (eliminating "Save/Apply" buttons in favor of transactional real-time mutation), and replacing indirect form controls with tactile, physics-backed canvas manipulation.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Why am I typing a number into a text box and clicking an 'Update Preview' button like I'm submitting a punch card to an IBM 360 in 1967? Let me grab the curve on the canvas and pull it! When I pull it, the data mutates at 120 frames per second under my fingertips. When I release it, it commits. Delete the 'Apply' button. Delete the 'Edit Mode' toggle. Separate 'View' and 'Edit' modes are for cowards who couldn't figure out clean focus states. The content IS the control."*
