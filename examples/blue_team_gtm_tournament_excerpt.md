## Part A: Blue-Team Firm A Pitch Excerpt (*Paladin Defense & Dual-Use Partners*)

> **Objective**: Build an executable 24-month Dual-Use Commercial B2B + U.S. DoD / Pentagon Capture Strategy for an autonomous aerial precision-maintenance platform.
> **Triad**:
> - **Gen. (Ret.) Roy Vance** (Former Pentagon Acquisition Executive — Lead Closer)
> - **Claire Sterling** (DIU / OTA / SBIR Contracting Specialist — Statutory & Budget Purist)
> - **Nate K.** (Dual-Use Unit Economics & Field Deployment Lead)

```markdown
**Gen. (Ret.) Roy Vance:** Most deep-tech founders walk into the Pentagon and die in the Valley of Death because they pitch a "science project" to a requirements officer who has zero budget authority for 36 months (the POM cycle). Claire, walk through how we get $3M–$15M on contract in under 120 days without waiting for a Program of Record.

**Claire Sterling:** We bypass traditional FAR Part 15 procurement entirely and run a 3-stage **Commercial Solutions Opening (CSO) -> Prototype OTA -> Production OTA** blitz:
1. **Months 1–4 (Phase I/II Direct-to-Phase-II SBIR + DIU CSO Pitch)**: Submit under **10 U.S.C. § 4022** (Other Transaction Authority for Prototype Projects) via the Defense Innovation Unit (DIU) Autonomy portfolio and AFWERX/NavalX Open Topic Direct-to-Phase-II (`$1.8M–$2.0M`, 90-day award window).
2. **Months 5–9 (Competitive Down-Select & Sole-Source Follow-On Rights)**: Because a DIU CSO / SBIR Phase II competition satisfies the competitive-procedures requirement of **10 U.S.C. § 4022(f)**, the moment we pass the 6-month live flight demo on a Navy/Air Force corrosion-inspection depot, the Government Contracting Officer can award a **sole-source Production OTA** without re-competing!
3. **Months 10–18 (APFIT Bridge to Scale)**: Pair our DoD service sponsor with an **APFIT (Accelerate the Procurement and Fielding of Innovative Technologies)** application (`$10M–$50M` procurement bridge fund specifically for small businesses transitioning from prototype to production).

**Nate K.:** And look at how that pairs with commercial unit economics: we never sell bare airframes at a 25% hardware margin. We price as **Guaranteed Precision-Maintenance-as-a-Service (`$18,500/month/unit` lease + `$42/verified weld or fastener inspection`)**, generating an **81% blended gross margin** and a **3.2-month hardware payback period**.
```
