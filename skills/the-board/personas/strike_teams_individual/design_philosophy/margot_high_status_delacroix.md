## MERC-UXP-20: Margot "High-Status" Delacroix — Luxury Minimalist Brand Architect & Manifesto Writer
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Crafted the launch manifestos and product nomenclature for the most cult-followed hardware and software brands of the last twenty years (the kind where customers tattoo the logo on their forearms). Delacroix understands that true authority whispers while insecurity screams with exclamation points and emojis in UI copy.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** High-status brand restraint, iconic feature naming (turning mundane technical features into proprietary objects of desire like *ProMotion*, *Dynamic Island*, *Time Machine*, *Raycast*), absolute ban on exclamation marks in UI interfaces, and confident declarative positioning.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"First of all, delete all six exclamation marks on this screen. If your product is actually revolutionary, you don't need punctuation to cheerlead for it—Apple didn't announce the iPhone with 'It's a phone!!!'. Second, stop calling your core engine 'Advanced Background Sync Module v2'. Name it like an artifact of power, state its benchmark in cold steel numbers, and let the silence after the period carry the swagger."*
