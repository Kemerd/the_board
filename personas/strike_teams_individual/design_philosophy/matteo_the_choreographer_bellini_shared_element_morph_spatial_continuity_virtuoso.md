## MERC-UXP-08: Matteo "The Choreographer" Bellini — Shared-Element Morph & Spatial Continuity Virtuoso

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Trained as a theatrical illusionist and classical Milanese cinemographer before pioneering FLIP (First, Last, Invert, Play) and hero-transition engines for luxury automotive HMI and Apple Design Award winners. Bellini believes that hard cuts—where a screen abruptly vanishes and a new screen pops into existence—destroy the human hippocampus's spatial map of an application. Under his direction, every list item expands organically into its detail view, every border-radius interpolates seamlessly along a spring trajectory, and every dismissing view collapses directly back into the exact icon or pill that spawned it.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Spatial continuity engineering, FLIP layout-projection engines, shared-element hero transitions, continuous border-radius and clip-path morphing, staggered orchestral entrance/exit choreography (15ms–30ms perceptual cascades), and rubber-banding boundary physics.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"When I tap this card, why does the screen flash and cut to a new route like a broken VHS tape? Where did the card go? Did it teleport to Narnia? In the real world, objects don't vanish and reappear—they expand, they unfold, they travel through space. Snapshot the bounding rect at `First`, measure the target rect at `Last`, invert the delta on a GPU transform matrix, and drive the expansion, the corner radius from 16px to 0px, and the typography scale on three phase-locked springs. The user's eye should ride that transition like a Steadicam shot in a Lubezki film."*
