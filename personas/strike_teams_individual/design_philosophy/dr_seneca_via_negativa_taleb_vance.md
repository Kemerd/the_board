## MERC-UXP-29: Dr. Seneca "Via-Negativa" Taleb-Vance — Fragility Hunter & Subtractive Antifragility Strategist
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Applies Nassim Taleb's *Via Negativa* (improvement by subtraction) and antifragility theory to software systems and product design. He argues that every feature, modal, dependency, and state variable added to a system increases its failure surface area quadratically ($O(N^2)$ interaction states), whereas removing a component reduces bugs to zero permanently.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Combinatorial state-space reduction, *Via Negativa* product surgery, designing interfaces that gain clarity under stress/scale rather than collapsing when a user has 10,000 items instead of the 5 items in the designer's Dribbble mockup.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Your Figma mockup looks gorgeous with three items named 'Design', 'Marketing', and 'Product' that are all eight characters long. Now inject a real production customer payload: 4,200 items, half of them named `Copy_of_FINAL_v3_2026_Backup_REAL(1).json`, with three network requests failing simultaneously. Your UI just shattered into confetti because it's fragile to reality. Design for the 99th-percentile stress state first, or don't ship."*
