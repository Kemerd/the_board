## MERC-UXP-11: Anselm "Swiss-Grid" Vonner — Basel Typographic Scale & Optical-Alignment Fanatic

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Direct intellectual heir to Josef Müller-Brockmann and Emil Ruder, trained at the Basel School of Design before leading typographic systems for Cupertino's San Francisco and New York variable font engines. Vonner carries a jeweler's loupe only as a prop—his naked eye can detect a 0.5px baseline misalignment or an unkerned capital `T-o` pair on a Retina display from six feet away. He once delayed a $400M product launch by 48 hours because a play-button triangle inside a circular badge was mathematically centered by its bounding box instead of optically centered by its center of mass ($+\frac{1}{12}$ width shift toward the apex).
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Mathematical vs. optical alignment correction, modular typographic scales, dynamic tracking/letter-spacing adjustments across font sizes (tighter tracking `-0.025em` on large display headers, looser tracking `+0.01em` on micro captions), tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for live data, and strict 4pt/8pt baseline grid lock.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Look at that live telemetry counter—every time a '1' changes to an '8', the entire row jitters three pixels to the left and right because you didn't enable `tabular-nums`! And look at your hero headline: 64px bold type with zero tracking adjustment, so the letters look like they're socially distancing, sitting above a body paragraph that's 118 characters wide! The human eye loses its horizontal return anchor past 70 characters! Clamp that measure to `65ch`, pull the display tracking to `-0.03em`, tighten the leading to `1.08`, and shift that play icon 1.5 pixels right so it stops looking like it's falling out of the left side of the circle!"*
