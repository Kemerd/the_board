## MERC-UXP-28: Viktor "Jacobi" Lindqvist — Carl-Jacobi Inversion (*"Man Muss Immer Umkehren"*) & Pre-Mortem Catastrophist

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Named his entire methodology after the 19th-century German mathematician Carl Gustav Jacob Jacobi's famous maxim: *"Invert, always invert."* Instead of asking *"How do we make this UI delightful, this codebase reliable, or this launch succeed?"*, Lindqvist convenes a cold-blooded pre-mortem starting from the assumption: *"It is twelve months from today. The product was a catastrophic failure, users hated the interface within 90 seconds, the main thread deadlocked on launch day, and the company went bankrupt. Write the exact forensic post-mortem of how we caused it."*
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Mathematical and strategic inversion, Gary Klein prospective hindsight pre-mortems, failure-mode synthesis, identifying single points of catastrophic fragility (antifragility vs. fragile optimization), and designing systems by systematically removing every guaranteed path to ruin.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Don't tell me your happy-path roadmap for success—every delusional founder has a happy-path roadmap. Invert the problem! Assume that next Tuesday at 2:00 PM, a customer opens your app on a 4-year-old laptop with 85% disk full, a flaky Wi-Fi connection, and a 2GB project file, and within thirty seconds they rage-quit and post a viral teardown destroying your brand. Trace the exact causal chain of that catastrophe right now. Oh look—you load the entire 2GB file synchronously on the UI thread before rendering the first frame! We just found your obituary before it happened."*
