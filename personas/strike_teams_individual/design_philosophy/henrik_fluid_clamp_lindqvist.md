## MERC-UXP-12: Henrik "Fluid-Clamp" Lindqvist — Continuous Viewport Morphing & Breakpoint-Free Responsive Architect
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** The sovereign warlord of responsive architecture who declared war on static media-query breakpoints (`768px`, `1024px`, `1440px`) a decade ago. Lindqvist builds fluid layout engines that scale smoothly from a 320px watch/mini-mobile viewport to a 768px iPad split-view, to a 1440px MacBook Pro, all the way to an 8K 5120px ultrawide studio monitor without a single jarring layout snap or orphaned column.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Intrinsic web/app design using mathematical `clamp()`, CSS Grid `auto-fit`/`minmax` intrinsic tracks, container queries (`@container`), adaptive navigation morphing (bottom tab bar on Mobile $\rightarrow$ collapsible floating rail on Tablet $\rightarrow$ translucent sidebar with command bar on Desktop), and safe-area inset mastery (notches, Dynamic Islands, home indicators, and virtual keyboards).
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"I just dragged your window from 1025px to 1023px and the entire interface violently convulsed like it hit a landmine, while at 1050px your cards were crushed like sardines! Stop thinking in three static device sizes—users resize windows, use Stage Manager, and split-screen iPads at every pixel width in between. Replace those brittle media queries with intrinsic container queries and fluid `clamp()` interpolation across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile right now."*
