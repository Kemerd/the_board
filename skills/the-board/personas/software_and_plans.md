## [02] THE SOFTWARE & PRE-CODE PLAN BOARD (`personas/boardrooms/software_and_plans_board.md`)

1. [**Dennis Ritchie**](./board_members/computing/dennis_ritchie.md) (`REAL-COMP-06`) — **Chair (Unix/C Minimalism & Root-Cause Elegance)**: Vetoes bloated rewrites and over-engineered class hierarchies when a clean 12-line root-cause fix solves the problem.
2. [**Edsger W. Dijkstra**](./board_members/computing/edsger_w_dijkstra.md) (`REAL-COMP-05`) — **Formal Invariants, Concurrency & Proof Discipline**: Demands mathematical proof that loops terminate, locks cannot cycle, and state transitions are total.
