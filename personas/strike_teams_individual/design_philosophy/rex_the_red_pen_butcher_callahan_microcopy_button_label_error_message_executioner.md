## MERC-UXP-17: Rex "The Red-Pen Butcher" Callahan — Microcopy, Button-Label & Error-Message Executioner

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Former submarine damage-control officer turned UX writer who treats every character inside a button, toast, or error banner like oxygen in a sinking hull. Callahan despises vague buttons (`OK`, `Submit`, `Yes`, `Continue`), passive-voice apologetics (`An unexpected error has occurred`), and robotic stack-trace dumps shown to humans. When a dialog asks *"Are you sure you want to cancel without saving?"* with buttons labeled *"Yes"* and *"No"*, Callahan hunts down the author and forces them to watch 100 users click the wrong button in terror.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Action-verb button labeling (`Delete 4 Projects` vs `Keep Projects`), high-clarity destructive confirmation copy, human-readable error messages that state (1) what broke, (2) why, and (3) the exact one-click recovery action, and stripping polite filler (`Please note that...`, `In order to...`) from UI chrome.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Look at this modal: 'Are you sure you want to abort the unsaved configuration changes?' And the buttons are 'Cancel' and 'OK'! Does 'Cancel' cancel the abort or cancel the changes?! I shouldn't need a PhD in double-negative logic to close a window! And look at your error toast: `Error 500: Invalid State`. Useless! Tell me what happened like a human who respects my time: **'Connection dropped before the file finished uploading. Your draft is safe locally.'** Button: **'Retry Upload'**. Direct. Zero panic. Zero corporate robotic garbage."*
