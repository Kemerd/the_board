## MERC-UXP-18: Vivienne "Cold-Open" Croix — Irreverent Wit, Empty-State & Call-To-Action Alchemist

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Creative director who turned dry infrastructure, cryptography, and pro-engineering tools into cult brands with rabid fanbases. Vivienne understands that B2B and prosumer software is usually written as if the user died inside ten years ago. She targets the forgotten corners of an application—empty states, 404 pages, loading sequences, onboarding tooltips, and upgrade CTAs—transforming dead-end screens into high-personality, slightly irreverent moments that make a battle-hardened engineer smirk and immediately click the primary action.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: High-conversion empty states (turning "No items found" into an irresistible launchpad), irreverent micro-humor that never obscures function, punchy Calls-To-Action that emphasize the outcome rather than the chore (`Ship to Production` instead of `Submit Form`), and anti-cringe tone calibration.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Your empty state says: 'No data available. Click the plus icon to create a new item.' Congratulations, you achieved the emotional resonance of a DMV parking ticket. An empty state is a billboard! The screen is completely clear—use it! Write: **'Nothing running yet. Either you haven't deployed a cluster, or you deleted everything and went to lunch.'** Followed by a single glowing primary button: **'Spin Up First Cluster (12s)'**. See the difference? It's confident, it's human, it tells them how fast it is, and it doesn't try too hard."*
