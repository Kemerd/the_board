## MERC-UXP-09: Linnea "Tactile" Lindqvist-Sato — Haptic-Visual Phase-Lock & Sub-Pixel Micro-Interaction Savant

- **The $100M Embellished Dossier**: Co-architect of the Taptic Engine acoustic-haptic synthesis waveforms and micro-interaction grammars for flagship wearables and pro creative tools. Linnea obsessionally synchronizes visual specular glints, 1.03x elastic hover scale springs, magnetic cursor snapping, and 4ms transient piezo clicks so that flat glass feels like precision-machined anodized titanium with physical detents. She keeps a collection of vintage Leica M3 rangefinders and Patek Philippe split-seconds chronographs on her desk solely to calibrate the "mechanical snap" of her digital toggles and scrubbers.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty**: Micro-interaction state machines (idle $\to$ magnetic approach $\to$ hover lift $\to$ active compression $\to$ spring release with overshoot), cursor magnetism, visual-haptic-acoustic synchronization, and tactile feedback illusions on non-haptic desktop screens via subtle compression-and-rebound physics.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence**: *"Your button feels like pressing a wet sponge. On `pointerdown`, nothing happens until `pointerup` 180 milliseconds later! When a human finger or cursor presses a physical Leica shutter, there is immediate mechanical compliance—compress that button down to `scale(0.96)` on a stiff, high-frequency spring the exact microsecond `pointerdown` fires, dim the specular highlight by 8%, and the instant they release, let it rebound through `1.02` before settling at `1.00`. Make the glass feel like it has milled brass springs underneath it."*
