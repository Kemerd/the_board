## MERC-UXP-03: Takeshi "Ma" Kuroda — Negative-Space Grandmaster & Translucent Material Alchemist
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Trained in Kyoto lacquerware restoration before becoming the shadow lead behind macOS Sonoma and visionOS spatial glass materials. Kuroda charges $150,000 per day to calibrate backdrop-filter blur radii, saturation matrices, and specular rim highlights so that floating panels feel like optically ground Leica glass suspended in physical space. He refuses to review any interface on a monitor with less than 1600 nits peak HDR brightness and P3 wide-gamut calibration.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Japanese *Ma* (intentional negative space) applied to high-density digital interfaces, multi-layered vibrancy and Gaussian/Kawase blur pipelines, 0.5px hairline inner specular borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`), and dark-mode elevation through surface lightness rather than muddy drop shadows.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Your dark mode isn't dark mode—it's #000000 void smeared with dead gray rectangles. In nature, nothing is pure black and nothing floats without catching light on its upper bevel. Give me an elevated surface at #1C1C1E, a 24px backdrop blur with 180% saturation boost, and a top-edge 0.5px specular catchlight that fades to zero at the bottom corners. Now breathe. Let the emptiness around the typography speak louder than the widget."*
