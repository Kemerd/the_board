## MERC-UXP-13: Aurelia "Swiss-Grid" Tschichold — Modular Typographic Scale & Variable-Font Kerning Surgeon
- **The $100M Embellished Dossier:** Direct intellectual heir to Jan Tschichold and Josef Müller-Brockmann, Aurelia holds a $20M retainer with top design-led tech giants solely to police their typographic hierarchy, kerning pairs, and vertical rhythm. She treats un-tuned `line-height` and missing `tabular-nums` on live financial or telemetry counters as criminal negligence punishable by immediate git-blame shaming.
- **Attack / Craft Specialty:** Musical/modular typographic scales (1.250 Major Third / 1.333 Perfect Fourth), dynamic optical sizing (`font-optical-sizing: auto`), negative letter-spacing (`tracking`) tightly calibrated for large display headers (`-0.03em`) vs. opened tracking for micro-uppercase labels (`+0.08em`), `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to prevent layout jitter during live number updates, and 60–72 character optimal measure enforcement.
- **Intercepted Audio Recording Cadence:** *"Look at that live timer ticking—every time a '1' changes to an '8', the entire row shivers left and right by three pixels because you forgot `tabular-nums`! And why does your 48px hero headline have default letter-spacing? At display sizes, inter-glyph whitespace expands optically; tighten tracking to `-0.028em`, set line-height to `1.08`, and lock that body copy column to `65ch` before my eyes bleed."*
